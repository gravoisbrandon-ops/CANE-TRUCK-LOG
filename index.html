Cane tools

Farms

Every farm is separate, with its own trucks, drivers, combine operator, loads and report. Administrators can open any farm and switch between them. Crew log only on their own farm. A farm with two or more mills asks the driver which mill the loads are going to. Each new date starts with a clean list.

People

Crew make their own account from your invite link. A crew manager can add and remove trucks, edit mills, tracks and fields, and manage the crew on their farm. Crew can log loads and add trucks.

Scale connection

Give each farm's key to whoever runs your scale app. It sends each net weight (tons, and TRS if it has it) and the quota, and they fill in on the Board and in the reports. Truck numbers must match the truck numbers here.

Trucks

Add, change and remove trucks right on the Board, with the Add truck and Edit trucks and mills buttons. Use this only to see the full list or to add a hauler name.
